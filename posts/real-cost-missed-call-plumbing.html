The real cost of a missed call for a plumbing business

September 7, 2026

Most plumbers think about a missed call as one lost job. Maybe $300. Maybe $500. They shrug it off — they're busy anyway, there'll be another call tomorrow.

But that's not how the math actually works.

The real number is much higher

That caller who didn't get through? They found someone else. And when that job went well, they left a 5-star review for the other guy. They recommended him to their neighbor. They called him again when the water heater went out six months later.

One missed call doesn't cost you $300. It costs you $300 plus the lifetime value of that customer — referrals, repeat jobs, reviews — which can easily reach into the thousands.

You can't answer when you're working

This isn't about being careless. A plumber under a sink can't pick up the phone. An electrician inside a panel can't stop mid-job to take a call. The nature of the work makes it physically impossible to be available every time someone calls.

The old solution was a receptionist. But a part-time receptionist costs $1,500 to $2,000 a month once you factor in taxes, training, and the inevitable days they're not there. And when they're sick, calls still go unanswered.

What changes with an AI voice agent

An AI agent answers every call. Every single one. At 2pm on a Tuesday and at 11pm on a Saturday. It captures the caller's name, number, and what they need. It can qualify the job — emergency or scheduled, residential or commercial. And it follows up automatically so nothing falls through the cracks.

You still do all the actual plumbing. You just stop losing the jobs you never even knew you were losing.
